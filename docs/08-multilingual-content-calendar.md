## Hreflang

Use:
- `en-LK`
- `ta-LK`
- `si-LK`
- `x-default`

Do not force IP-based redirects. Keep a visible language switcher.

## Translation quality rules

Do not:
- machine translate hundreds of pages and index them without review
- translate only navigation while body remains English
- use unnatural formal Tamil that local customers do not use

Do:
- preserve common automotive English terms where natural
- localize CTAs
- localize FAQs
- keep brand names exact
- have a local native reviewer

# Content calendar

## Principle

Google’s current AI-search guidance emphasizes first-hand, non-commodity content. Therefore IET should publish real workshop knowledge rather than generic “10 tips” content.

## Recommended cadence

- 2 strong knowledge articles/month
- 2–4 case studies/job posts/month
- weekly Google Business Profile photo/update
- monthly founder/mentor expertise post
- quarterly partnership/company update

Earn mentions from:

## Social → website loop

- service reel → service page
- genuine parts post → parts page
- anniversary → milestone page
- founder post → founder page
- future vision → expansion/partners page
- technical tip → knowledge article

This grows branded demand and reinforces entity relationships.
