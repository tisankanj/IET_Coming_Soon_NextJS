## Critical brand boundary

The current TVS authorization should be described as **three-wheeler authorization**. Do not imply TVS motorcycle authorization unless IET receives that authorization in writing.

If IET genuinely services motorcycles/bikes independently, target motorcycle/bike repair searches through separate service pages while clearly separating those services from TVS three-wheeler authorization.

## Ranking objective

The goal is to maximize relevance, local prominence, crawlability, authority, conversion and AI citation likelihood for consumer and B2B queries. No legitimate SEO method can guarantee “#1 in every search engine or AI answer.” The measurable objective is to increase local-pack visibility, organic clicks, AI citations, branded demand, reviews, service bookings, genuine-parts enquiries and partnership leads.
