# 00 — Executive Strategy

## North-star objective

Build `ietservice.lk` into the strongest locally relevant first-party web entity for **three-wheeler service, repair, maintenance and TVS genuine parts in Jaffna**, then extend that authority across Northern Province and Sri Lanka.

At the same time, use the website as a **B2B credibility and partnership engine** so vehicle manufacturers, motorcycle and three-wheeler brands, oil/lubricant companies, spare-parts distributors, tool/equipment suppliers, fleet operators and dealer networks can discover IET and assess it as a Northern Province partner.

## Search growth order

1. Own branded entity searches for **IET Service Point**.
2. Build strong relevance for **Inuvil**.
3. Compete for **Jaffna** commercial queries.
4. Extend to genuine service areas across **Jaffna District**.
5. Build authority for **Northern Province** automotive service.
6. Build national visibility for B2B partnership and selected automotive topics.

## What will actually improve SEO and AI visibility

Current Google guidance for generative AI search says normal SEO remains foundational. It strongly favors unique, useful, first-hand, non-commodity content and warns against mass-generating near-duplicate query pages.

For IET, the best authority assets are:

- real workshop jobs and case studies
- real service checklists
- real problems seen in Jaffna vehicles
- before/after repair evidence
- genuine TVS parts verification guides using original photos
- real pricing where stable
- technician demonstrations
- founder and mentor expertise
- customer stories and reviews
- service-camp/event coverage
- TVS authorization evidence
- real parts availability content
- Northern Sri Lanka maintenance advice
- commercial-driver maintenance advice
