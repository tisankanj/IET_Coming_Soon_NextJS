# 03 — Local SEO: Jaffna and Northern Province

## Google Business Profile

Google requires the profile to represent the real-world business accurately. Keep the name exactly:

**IET Service Point**

Do not use a keyword-stuffed name such as:

**IET Service Point – Best TVS Three Wheeler Service Jaffna**

## Storefront / hybrid setup

Because IET operates a physical garage where customers can visit, represent it as a storefront. If IET also visits customers or offers roadside/mobile service, it may qualify as a hybrid business and can add accurate service areas.

Google currently allows up to 20 service areas and recommends specific real areas rather than a radius.

## Category strategy

Choose the closest actual primary category available in Google Business Profile for Sri Lanka. Add secondary categories only for real services.

Potential categories to evaluate in the live GBP UI:
- Auto repair shop
- Motorcycle repair shop — only if actual
- Auto parts store — if parts are sold to walk-in customers
- relevant dealer category if available and accurate

Do not add categories only because they contain keywords.

## GBP services

Where available, add:
- Three-Wheeler General Service
- Oil Service
- Greasing
- Brake Service
- Electrical Repair
- Engine Inspection
- Suspension & Steering

Do not put phone numbers, “best”, discounts or promotional wording inside service names.

## Review acquisition

Ask after a successful service, repair or genuine-parts purchase.

Suggested text:

> Thank you for visiting IET Service Point. If you were happy with our service, your Google review would help more customers in Jaffna find us.

Never buy reviews.

## Review response framework

1. Thank the customer.
2. Mention the real service if public.
3. Mention IET naturally.
4. Avoid keyword stuffing.
5. Invite them back.

## Local citation priorities

Prefer trusted, correct citations over hundreds of weak links:
- official brand/dealer networks
- credible Sri Lankan business directories
- local chamber/trade organizations
- local news sites
- automotive portals
- maps/directories
- supplier partner websites
- training/technical organizations

## Competitive insight from live research

Public Jaffna search results show multiple motorcycle/service/spares businesses, including TVS-related and multi-brand workshops, often surfaced through directories such as LankaGuide/Cybo and brand networks such as DPMC. This suggests a fragmented local digital market where a strong first-party site can differentiate through:

- clear local service pages
- authorization details
- genuine-parts verification
- online booking
- English/Tamil/Sinhala
- current reviews
- real workshop photos
- first-hand technical content
- B2B partnership pages

## Location page evidence rule

Before publishing a dedicated town/district page, have at least two:
- real jobs from that area
- customer review from that area
- delivery/service logistics
- route information
- local photos
- actual service-area relevance
- specific parts/service demand

Otherwise keep the place inside the broader Jaffna/Northern page.
