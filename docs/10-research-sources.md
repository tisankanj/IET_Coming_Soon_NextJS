## Important 2026 findings

1. Google says SEO best practices remain foundational for AI Overviews/AI Mode.
2. Google’s current AI guide emphasizes unique, first-hand, non-commodity content.
3. Google specifically warns against mass-producing a page for every query variation.
4. Google says `llms.txt` is not needed for Google Search and does not improve Google rankings/AI visibility.
5. Google removed FAQ rich-result display in 2026; visible FAQs remain useful content.
6. Google now provides Generative AI performance reporting in Search Console.
7. OpenAI says public sites can appear in ChatGPT Search and recommends allowing OAI-SearchBot.
8. Bing Webmaster Tools now provides AI citation/grounding visibility.
9. Google Business Profile requires accurate real-world names and service areas.
10. TVS’s official Sri Lanka content emphasizes authorized service networks and genuine-parts verification.
11. Jaffna SERPs show many workshop/service competitors surfaced through directories, leaving room for a stronger first-party local website.

## Site availability observation

During this research, a direct fetch of `https://ietservice.lk` returned a **502 Bad Gateway** response. It may have been transient. Verify uptime, DNS/origin health, Cloudflare behavior and crawlability before the SEO launch.

## Important strategic interpretation

The strongest path is not “SEO hacks”. It is:

- accurate business entity
- crawlable technical foundation
- strong local profile
- real reviews
- original workshop evidence
- clear three-wheeler authorization
- genuine-parts authority
- Tamil + English relevance
- founder/mentor expertise
- supplier/dealership-facing corporate pages
- consistent updates and measurement
