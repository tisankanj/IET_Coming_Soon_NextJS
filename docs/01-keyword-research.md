# 01 — Keyword Research and Search Intent Map

## Method and limitation

This keyword plan combines live web/SERP sampling, local Jaffna directory evidence, TVS terminology, official search-engine guidance and commercial intent analysis.

**No proprietary Google Ads, Semrush or Ahrefs search-volume database was available in this research environment. No fake volume numbers are included.**

After launch, reprioritize using:

- Google Search Console queries
- Google Search Console Generative AI performance
- Bing Webmaster Tools Keyword Research
- Bing AI Performance grounding queries
- Google Business Profile Performance
- Google Ads Keyword Planner if ads are used

## Opportunity snapshot

Public results around Jaffna show multiple motorcycle/service/spares businesses surfaced mainly through directories and local listings. That creates a practical opening for IET to win with a strong first-party website, clear authorization details, original photos, online booking, tri-language content and current reviews.

## Priority model

- **P0:** money keywords + core local entity
- **P1:** high-value supporting commercial queries
- **P2:** regional/national expansion and informational queries
- **Conditional:** only publish if the service is genuinely offered

## Keyword-to-page rule

One strong page should rank for many related queries. Do not create separate pages such as:

- `/best-three-wheeler-service-jaffna/`
- `/cheap-three-wheeler-service-jaffna/`
- `/top-three-wheeler-service-jaffna/`

Instead create one authoritative service page, one strong Jaffna page, relevant FAQs, original evidence and internal links.
